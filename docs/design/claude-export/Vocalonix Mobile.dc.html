What changed for the phone
Three columns become tabs The desktop rail is a four-tab bar; the right-hand detail column becomes a pushed screen with a back link. Nothing is a modal, because a modal on a phone hides the thing you were reading.
The diary loses its grid Four resource columns won’t survive 390px, so time runs down the page and the person becomes a filter chip. Dragging is replaced by ±10 minute nudges and a person picker.
One action per screen, at the bottom Ring, done, republish — the primary action sits in thumb reach above the tab bar, and every tap target clears 44px. Outcomes stay buttons, never typed notes.
